Model Evaluation Report

Performance metrics calculated on the 20% unseen test split of the ISOT Fake & Real News Dataset.

Accuracy 98.7% Overall correct predictions
Precision 98.6% Exactness of positive predictions
Recall 98.8% Coverage of actual real articles
F1-Score 98.7% Harmonic mean of precision & recall

Technical Note on Dataset Bias: While the model achieves high statistical accuracy, the ISOT news dataset exhibits strong stylistic markers (e.g., real news frequently begins with wire service tags such as WASHINGTON (Reuters), whereas fake news often contains sensational headlines and informal phrasing).
